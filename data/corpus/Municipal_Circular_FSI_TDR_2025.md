# Municipal Circular on Floor Area Ratio, TDR and Setbacks

Circular No. BBMP/TP/2025/117 dated 21 April 2025, Town Planning Department. Fictional sample data for the Satyori demo.

## 1. Base floor area ratio

The base floor area ratio (FAR, also called floor space index or FSI) for residential plots abutting roads narrower than 12 metres is 1.75. For plots abutting roads of 12 metres to 18 metres the base FAR is 2.25. For plots abutting roads wider than 18 metres the base FAR is 2.5. Basement parking does not count towards FAR.

## 2. Transferable development rights

Owners who surrender land for road widening receive transferable development rights (TDR) equal to 1.5 times the area surrendered. TDR may be loaded on a receiving plot up to a maximum of 0.4 additional FAR for roads under 18 metres and 0.6 additional FAR for wider roads. TDR certificates must be registered with the Sub-Registrar within 90 days of issue.

## 3. Setbacks and height

For buildings taller than 15 metres, the minimum front setback is 6 metres and side and rear setbacks are one fifth of the building height, subject to a minimum of 5 metres. Any building taller than 21 metres requires a fire no objection certificate from the Karnataka State Fire and Emergency Services before the building plan is sanctioned.

## 4. Lake buffer zones

No construction other than landscaping is allowed within 30 metres of the boundary of any lake, as directed by the National Green Tribunal. Compound walls, roads and swimming pools are treated as construction for this purpose.

## 5. Occupancy certificate

An occupancy certificate must be obtained before any unit is handed over. Water, electricity and sewage connections for residential use shall not be sanctioned without a valid occupancy certificate. Promoters handing over possession without an occupancy certificate are liable to a penalty of up to 10 percent of the project cost.
