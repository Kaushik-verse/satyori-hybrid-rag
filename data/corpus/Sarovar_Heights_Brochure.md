# Sarovar Heights: Sales Brochure

Lakeside living, reimagined. A marketing brochure from Nirmaan Infra Developers LLP. Fictional sample data for the Satyori demo.

## 1. The address

Sarovar Heights sits beside the Hennur lake, just 5 minutes from the upcoming metro station and 25 minutes from Kempegowda International Airport. Top schools, hospitals and tech parks are a short drive away, making it the perfect home for growing families.

## 2. Possession

Move in by December 2026. Our track record of on-time delivery means you can plan your housewarming with confidence. Early buyers receive a complimentary modular kitchen worth Rs 3.5 lakh.

## 3. Homes

Spacious 2 BHK homes from 1,150 sq ft and 3 BHK homes from 1,560 sq ft (super built-up area). Every home has an east or north facing balcony with lake views, vitrified flooring, and branded sanitary ware.

## 4. Amenities

A 40,000 sq ft clubhouse, a rooftop infinity pool, a half olympic lap pool, an indoor badminton court, a co-working lounge with high-speed internet, a pet park, and a 1.2 km jogging track around landscaped gardens. Electric vehicle charging is provided in every parking bay.

## 5. Pricing and offers

Prices start at Rs 89 lakh for a 2 BHK, all inclusive. Pay just 10 percent now and nothing until possession under our subvention plan. Zero stamp duty offer for bookings made before Diwali. Car parking is complimentary for all 3 BHK homes.
