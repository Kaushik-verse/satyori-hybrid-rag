# Encumbrance Certificate: Survey No. 88/2, Hennur Village

Form 15 encumbrance certificate issued by the Sub-Registrar, Hennur, for the period 1 April 2008 to 31 March 2025. EC number: HNR-EC-2025-004512. Fictional sample data for the Satyori demo.

## 1. Property schedule

All that piece and parcel of converted residential land bearing Survey No. 88/2, Hennur Village, Bengaluru East Taluk, measuring 3 acres 12 guntas, with Khata No. 1187/88/2 issued by the municipal corporation. Bounded on the north by the Hennur lake buffer, on the south by a 60 foot road, on the east by Survey No. 88/3 and on the west by Survey No. 87/1.

## 2. Registered transactions

Entry 1, 14 June 2009: Sale deed, document no. HNR-1-02231-2009-10, from Ramaiah Gowda to Lakshmamma, consideration Rs 42,00,000.

Entry 2, 2 February 2019: Joint development agreement, document no. HNR-1-07745-2018-19, between the landowner Lakshmamma and Nirmaan Infra Developers LLP, with a 40:60 built-up area sharing ratio in favour of the developer.

Entry 3, 11 August 2022: Mortgage by deposit of title deeds, document no. HNR-4-00918-2022-23, in favour of Kaveri Urban Co-operative Bank for a construction finance loan of Rs 38 crore.

Entry 4, 9 January 2025: Partial release deed, document no. HNR-4-00311-2024-25, releasing Tower A from the Kaveri Urban Co-operative Bank mortgage. Tower B remains mortgaged.

## 3. Observations for buyers

Because the mortgage over Tower B has not been released, any buyer of a Tower B apartment must obtain a no objection certificate from Kaveri Urban Co-operative Bank before the sale deed is registered. No lis pendens, court attachment or acquisition notice is recorded against the property during the search period.
