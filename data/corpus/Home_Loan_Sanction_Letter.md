# Home Loan Sanction Letter

Sanction reference: KUCB/HL/2025/BLR/77812, dated 3 March 2025, issued by Kaveri Urban Co-operative Bank, Hennur branch, for the purchase of Apartment B-1204, Sarovar Heights. Fictional sample data for the Satyori demo.

## 1. Loan terms

Sanctioned amount: Rs 1,02,00,000. Tenure: 240 months. Rate of interest: 8.65 percent per annum, floating, linked to the bank's repo-linked lending rate with a spread of 2.15 percent. Indicative equated monthly instalment: Rs 89,480. Processing fee: 0.35 percent of the sanctioned amount plus GST, non-refundable.

## 2. Loan to value

The loan is limited to 75 percent of the property cost as permitted by the Reserve Bank of India for loans above Rs 75 lakh. Stamp duty and registration charges are not included in the property cost for loan to value purposes.

## 3. Pre-disbursement conditions

Disbursement is construction-linked and will be released directly to the promoter's RERA designated account only. Before the first disbursement the borrower must submit the registered agreement for sale, an allotment letter, the RERA registration certificate, and a no objection certificate from any existing project lender. The sanction is valid for six months from the date of this letter.

## 4. Prepayment and foreclosure

No prepayment or foreclosure charges apply to floating rate home loans taken by individuals, in line with RBI guidelines. Part prepayments are allowed at any time with a minimum of one instalment.

## 5. Insurance

Property insurance against fire and natural calamities is mandatory for the full loan tenure. Credit life insurance is optional and must not be bundled as a condition of sanction.
