# Agreement for Sale: Apartment B-1204, Sarovar Heights

Draft agreement between Nirmaan Infra Developers LLP (the Promoter) and the Allottee for Apartment B-1204 on the 12th floor of Tower B. Fictional sample data for the Satyori demo.

## 1. The apartment and consideration

Apartment B-1204 is a three-bedroom unit with a RERA carpet area of 1,132 square feet, an exclusive balcony of 96 square feet, and one covered car parking slot numbered P2-117. The total consideration is Rs 1,38,60,000, which includes the car parking slot charge of Rs 4,50,000. The consideration excludes stamp duty, registration charges and GST, which are payable by the Allottee.

## 2. Payment plan

Ten percent of the consideration is payable on booking. Further instalments are linked to construction milestones: 20 percent on completion of the plinth, 10 percent on each slab group of six floors, and the final 15 percent on handover of possession together with the Occupancy Certificate. No instalment is payable before the milestone is certified by the project architect.

## 3. Possession date and delay compensation

The Promoter shall hand over possession on or before 30 June 2027. If the Promoter fails to deliver possession by this date, the Promoter shall pay the Allottee interest for every month of delay at the State Bank of India highest marginal cost of lending rate plus two percent per annum, as prescribed under the RERA Rules. A grace period of six months is available only for force majeure events such as war, flood, earthquake or a government-ordered stoppage of work.

## 4. Cancellation by the Allottee

The Allottee may cancel the booking at any time before possession. On cancellation, the Promoter may forfeit an amount equal to 10 percent of the total consideration as earnest money, and must refund the balance without interest within 45 days of the cancellation notice.

## 5. Maintenance and corpus deposit

On possession, the Allottee shall pay a one-time corpus fund deposit of Rs 150 per square foot of carpet area and advance maintenance charges for 12 months at Rs 4.50 per square foot per month. The Promoter shall transfer the corpus fund to the association of allottees within three months of its formation.

## 6. Defect liability

Any structural defect or defect in workmanship, quality or provision of services brought to the notice of the Promoter within five years from the date of handing over possession shall be rectified by the Promoter without further charge within 30 days, as required by Section 14(3) of the Act.
