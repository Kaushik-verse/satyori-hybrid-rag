# Amrapali Residency Apartment Owners Association: Bylaws (Extract)

Registered under the Karnataka Apartment Ownership Act, 1972, registration no. KAOA/BLR/2016/3321. Amrapali Residency is a completed 180-unit project in Indiranagar. Fictional sample data for the Satyori demo.

## 1. Maintenance charges

Monthly maintenance is charged at Rs 4.20 per square foot of super built-up area, payable by the 10th of each month. Late payment attracts simple interest of 18 percent per annum. A member with dues outstanding for more than 90 days loses voting rights and access to the clubhouse until the dues are cleared.

## 2. Sinking fund

Ten percent of all maintenance collections are credited to a sinking fund reserved for major repairs such as lift replacement, waterproofing and repainting. Withdrawals from the sinking fund need approval by two thirds of members present at a general body meeting.

## 3. Transfer of ownership

On sale of an apartment, the seller must obtain a no dues certificate from the association. The buyer pays a one-time transfer fee of Rs 25,000 to the association. The association cannot refuse to record a transfer, but may withhold the no dues certificate until all arrears are paid.

## 4. Renting and short stays

Owners may rent their apartments to tenants on leave and licence agreements of at least 11 months. Tenant details and police verification must be submitted to the association office before move-in. Short stays of less than 30 days, including holiday rentals, are prohibited.

## 5. Pets

Pets are permitted. Pet owners must leash dogs in common areas and use the service lift. As per the Animal Welfare Board of India guidelines, the association cannot ban pets or levy a separate pet fee.

## 6. Renovations

Interior renovation work is allowed only between 9 am and 6 pm on weekdays. Structural changes, removal of load-bearing walls and enclosing balconies are prohibited. A refundable damage deposit of Rs 20,000 is collected before any renovation starts.
