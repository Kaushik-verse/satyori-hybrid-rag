# Sarovar Heights: RERA Registration Certificate (Summary Extract)

Issued by the Real Estate Regulatory Authority under Section 5 of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016. This extract is fictional sample data for the Satyori demo.

## 1. Registration details

Project name: Sarovar Heights, Phase 1 (Towers A and B). Registration number: PRM/KA/RERA/1251/446/PR/180523/006789. The registration was granted on 18 May 2023 to the promoter Nirmaan Infra Developers LLP, LLPIN AAK-4471. The registration is valid until 30 June 2027, which is also the declared completion date for Phase 1. Any extension beyond this date requires a fresh application under Section 6 with reasons attributable to force majeure only.

## 2. Land and approvals

The project is built on Survey No. 88/2 of Hennur Village, Bengaluru East Taluk, measuring 3 acres 12 guntas. The building plan was sanctioned under LP No. BBMP/ADTP/0412/2022-23 for two towers of Ground plus 18 floors with 312 apartments in total. The land conversion order for residential use is DC/ALN/SR/233/2021.

## 3. Unit mix and carpet area

All areas are declared as RERA carpet area, meaning the net usable floor area excluding external walls, service shafts, exclusive balcony and open terrace. Two-bedroom units range from 812 to 846 square feet carpet area. Three-bedroom units range from 1,108 to 1,164 square feet carpet area. The promoter may not sell any unit on super built-up area alone and must state carpet area in every agreement.

## 4. Designated escrow account

As mandated by Section 4(2)(l)(D), seventy percent of all amounts realised from allottees must be deposited in the designated RERA escrow account no. 50200078123456 maintained with a scheduled bank. Withdrawals are permitted only in proportion to the percentage of completion, certified by an engineer, an architect and a chartered accountant.

## 5. Quarterly progress and complaints

The promoter must upload quarterly progress reports on the RERA portal. As of the March 2025 quarterly update, Tower A structural work was 72 percent complete and Tower B structural work was 41 percent complete. Allottees may file complaints with the Authority under Section 31. Interest payable by the promoter for delay is the State Bank of India highest marginal cost of lending rate plus two percent, as prescribed under Rule 16 of the Karnataka RERA Rules.
